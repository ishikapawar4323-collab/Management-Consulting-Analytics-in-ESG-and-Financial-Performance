## Executive Summary

Organizations increasingly invest in ESG initiatives, yet executives often struggle to quantify their financial impact. This project applies a **management consulting framework** to benchmark ESG maturity against financial performance across publicly listed companies, delivering executive dashboards and data-driven strategic insights.

---

## Business Problem

**Client challenge:** Can stronger ESG performance translate into measurable financial value?

This project answers that question by:

- Benchmarking ESG leaders and laggards
- Measuring relationships between ESG and profitability
- Identifying financial performance gaps
- Building executive-ready dashboards for decision makers

---

## Key Insights

- Higher ESG performance demonstrated a **moderate positive relationship** with long-term profitability.
- Companies with stronger ESG disclosure generally achieved better ROA and market valuation.
- Financial leverage remained a significant explanatory factor alongside ESG performance.
- Benchmarking revealed firms with strong financial results but comparatively weak ESG transparency.
